Platform truth

AlarmKit, not the Clock app.

Apple does not allow third-party apps to read or edit alarms in Clock. UpAlarm schedules alarms owned by UpAlarm through Apple’s AlarmKit framework.

Safety

You always keep control.

An emergency-dismiss route remains available if you are injured, unwell or the camera cannot count safely. UpAlarm records the result honestly instead of claiming alarms are impossible to dismiss.
